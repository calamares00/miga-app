What's new in version 1.0.35:
• Allergens: buckwheat, maize, rice or chickpea flour and lentils no longer show as "may contain gluten": they have none. The coeliac card only reminds you to buy them certified in case of traces.
• Cooking mode: times, temperatures and speeds are highlighted in the theme colour.
• Clearer recipe page: facts in one strip, allergens in one row, amounts in a column, highlighted times, smaller cooking mode button.
