First public release of Miga.
• Recipe books with photos, sub-recipes, optional ingredients and a cooking mode with timers and voice.
• Shopping list sorted by aisle, with several lists, templates and a product scanner (Open Food Facts).
• Optional cooking assistant with your own key (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI or OpenRouter): recipes from photos or websites, recipe improvements and ideas.
• Backups, recipe packs and syncing with your own server.
• In English and Spanish.
