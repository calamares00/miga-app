## 📚 Books and recipes

Each book is a recipe collection with its own cover: your mum's, a friend's or one per theme (the best brownies deserve a book of their own). New recipes are saved in the book you have open.

- In **Books**, the filters at the top (All, My books, Packs, Server) show each kind of book. You can also **swipe left or right** to move from one to the next.
- Long-press a book to **select it**, then tap others to add them. At the top you have Edit, Share and Delete, and under **More actions**, **Pin to top**: pinned books come first, with a pin on their cover. Inside a book you can also pin it from its menu (⋮).
- Inside a book, recipes are grouped by **category**. Tap a category to collapse or expand it; the menu (⋮) collapses or expands them all.
- When a recipe is open, **swipe left or right** to go to the next or previous recipe of the book.
- Long-press a recipe to **select several**. At the top you can edit it (if only one), share or delete them, and under **More actions** change their category, difficulty, servings or source at once, mark them as favourites, move or copy them to another book, add their ingredients to the shopping list or **polish them** ✨ all in one go.
- In each recipe, **My notes** keeps dated personal notes ("next time, less salt"). They do not change the recipe and go into backups.
- **Search** looks through every book by name or ingredient. The filter button narrows it down by time, difficulty, category, equipment, tags, ingredients or origin, and active filters show up as chips you can remove one by one. **Favourites** works the same, with your favourites only.

## ✍️ Writing a recipe

- **Ingredients**: as you type the name, the app suggests the ones you have used before. From each ingredient's menu (⋮) you can mark it as **optional** (parsley to garnish, for example): it shows "(optional)" without changing its name.
- **Sub-recipes**: split the recipe into parts (for example, "For the sauce"), each with its own ingredients and steps.
- **Steps and notes** support formatting: while you type, a bar offers **bold**, *italics*, strikethrough and lists. Pressing Enter in a list starts the next item.
- **Dictation**: tap the microphone to dictate the steps instead of typing them.
- **Origin**: where the recipe comes from (Mexico, Córdoba, Thai cuisine…). The flag appears on its own when the country is recognised; tap the globe or the flag to choose it.
- **Servings**: in the recipe, change the servings and the quantities are recalculated.
- **Photos**: when adding a photo you can crop it, rotate it and **enhance it** ✨ (light, colour and sharpness); it is saved at just the right size so it does not take up more space than needed.
- **Polish the recipe** ✨: from the recipe's menu (⋮), the assistant tidies up the wording, normalises the ingredients and highlights times and temperatures in bold. You see the before and after and decide whether to apply it; nothing is saved without your say-so.

## 📥 Adding recipes from other sources

From a book, tap **New recipe** and choose where it comes from:

- **Manual**: you write the recipe yourself.
- **From a file**: imports a Miga file (.miga or .zip) or a JSON export.
- **From an image** ✨: take a photo or pick one from the gallery. If the recipe continues on another page, add more photos. The assistant reads the text, crops the photo of the dish and fills in the editor so you only need to check it.
- **Several recipes from images** ✨: pick many photos and the app creates one recipe for each.
- **From a URL** ✨: paste the link to a recipe on any website.
- **Suggest a recipe** ✨: type the name of a dish and choose from the suggestions.

Options marked ✨ are done by the kitchen assistant and only appear once it is set up.

## 🍳 Cooking mode

In a recipe with steps, tap **Cooking mode** to see them one at a time in full screen, with the screen kept on. Tick off the ingredients as you use them.

- Times mentioned in a step ("simmer for 10 minutes") become one-tap **timers**.
- The app can **read each step aloud**. The voice is chosen in Settings → Voice and dictation.
- With the **voice command** (microphone) you can say "next", "previous", "repeat" or "timer".
- Turn on **hands-free** (the speaking-person icon) so it listens all the time: say "next" with your hands in the dough and it moves on without touching the phone. It stops listening while it reads a step aloud; to turn it off, tap the icon again.

## 🛒 Shopping list

- Add items by typing, **dictating** or **scanning the barcode**. From a recipe you can add all its ingredients at once, and the explore button opens the product **catalogue** and the Open Food Facts search.
- Items are grouped by category and sorted by **your supermarket's aisles**.
- You can have **several lists** and save **templates** for the weekly shop.
- **Swipe** an item right to tick it and left to remove it.
- **Shop mode** keeps only what you need while shopping and stops the screen from turning off.
- The **product sheet** (Open Food Facts) shows Nutri-Score, NOVA, Eco-Score, allergens, additives and an indicative score.
- Share the list as text or with a **QR code**, and use the **widget** to add things without opening the app.

## ✨ Ideas {ai}

Tap ✨ at the top of **Library** to ask for suggestions based on your recipes.

- Tap a quick idea ("What for breakfast?", weekly menu…) or combine options at the bottom: meal, style (healthy, quick, vegetarian, seasonal…), kind of dish and equipment, even a specific model such as "Thermomix TM31".
- You can also ask anything about cooking: techniques, substitutions, storage…
- Suggested recipes appear by name and open with a tap. You can add their ingredients to the shopping list, and from the **dishes you could add** create a new one in the book you choose.
- The assistant only talks about cooking and food, and never changes anything in the app.

## 📤 Sharing

**Share** is at the top of each recipe, in a book's menu (⋮) and at the top when recipes or books are selected. Choose how:

- **Send to someone**: a .miga file that opens with Miga on the other phone. It leaves out your favourites, ratings and notes.
- **Create link**: an encrypted link for WhatsApp, email…, which expires after 7 days.
- **PDF**: to read, print or send. It cannot be imported back into Miga.
- **Image** (one recipe): the whole recipe in one picture (photo, ingredients and steps), ready for Instagram or WhatsApp.
- **Text** (one recipe): to paste into a message.
- **Save a copy (.zip)**: with the photos, to import it into Miga later, on this phone or another.

Whoever receives a recipe chooses the book to keep it in; a book arrives as a new book or, if they already had it, is updated. Either way they see "Shared by" with the name you set in Settings → Your name.

## 👤 Your name

Signs what you share: shared shopping lists and the books and recipes you send. Whoever receives them sees "Shared by …".

## 🎨 Appearance

Light, dark or system theme, accent colour, app language and **photo frame**: choose one (Polaroid, gallery, vignette…) and every photo of your recipes matches.

## 🔒 Security

Turn on the **biometric lock** so the app asks for your fingerprint, face or PIN when it opens. If the phone has none set up, add one in Android's settings first.

## 🎙️ Voice and dictation

- **Dictation language**: the language used to recognise what you dictate (shopping list, recipe steps and cooking mode).
- **Voice**: the one that reads the steps aloud in cooking mode. Tap **Test voice** to hear it.

## 🗂️ Manage content

The lists of recipe categories, equipment, tags, ingredients and products, and product categories.

- Search, filter what is unused and sort by name or by use. Tap "N recipes" to see the recipes that use it.
- Renaming to a name that already exists **merges** the two, and the affected recipes are updated (in synced books too).
- **Clean up content** finds duplicates, names to unify and your own unused items, and tidies them up in one go. With the assistant on it is even sharper, and "Undo" is always there, just in case.

## 💾 Backup

- **Export the whole app** saves books, recipes and photos in one file, to restore it later on this phone or another.
- The backup can be **password-protected**: it is encrypted with AES-256 and only opens with that password. Miga does not store it anywhere, so if you forget it there is no way to recover the backup.
- **Import backup** restores a backup (ZIP, JSON or password-protected); if you like, it can delete what you have first.
- **Import recipes or a book** adds what a Miga file holds: one recipe, a selection or a whole book. For a book, it asks whether to add its recipes to one of your books or create it as a new book.
- While exporting or importing, Miga shows the progress (also in a notification, so you can keep using the app).

## 📊 Statistics

Figures about your recipes: recipes per book, category and difficulty, favourites, best rated and most cooked, times, most used ingredients and tags, world cuisines and recipes to complete (no photo, no steps…). Tap a figure to see its recipes. At the bottom you can see how much space Miga takes on the phone, with options to clear the cache and delete photos no recipe uses any more.

## 🔄 Sync

To share books and shopping lists with your family with full editing, connect Miga to a server you host yourself (miga-server). In **Manage connections** you add the server and namespace, and invite others with a QR code. Your data only goes to your server.

## 📦 Recipe packs

- **Explore catalogue**: books ready to install. A pack is installed as one more book; its recipes cannot be edited, but you can select them and copy them to one of your books.
- **Contribute to the install counter**: installing a pack from the official catalogue adds one anonymous install, to show which ones people like most. You can turn it off.
- **Alternative catalogue**: use another catalogue by entering its URL or a GitHub user/repo.

## 🤖 Kitchen assistant {ai}

Features marked ✨ are done by the kitchen assistant, which uses artificial intelligence (AI). They are optional and use **your own key** for Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI or OpenRouter (which has free models). The list of models loads on its own with your key.

- If you add several providers, they are used **in the order you choose**: if one fails, the next one is tried automatically.
- Each provider has a **switch**: turn it off whenever you like without losing its key.
- While it works, the app tells you **what it is doing** ("Reading the text in the photo…", "Going through your recipes…").
- If you leave the app or turn off the screen, the task **keeps going in the background** and a notification shows its progress. When a polish finishes, the notification takes you straight to the result to review it.
- The assistant can also rate how healthy a recipe is, estimate its nutrition and suggest **substitutes** for an ingredient (tap ⟳ next to the ingredient).
- AI-generated content is marked as such and may contain mistakes: check it. If something is inappropriate, use **Report AI content**.
