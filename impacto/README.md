# High-impact icons (proposals)

`propuestas.png`: 9 icons with vivid gradients, light and glow, each telling a part of the app
(something revealed under the cloche, the recipe book giving off aroma, a cooking video turned into a
recipe, cooking mode's fire, the assistant...). Each one is also here alone at 512×512. `make.py`
writes the HTML they were rendered from.
