Miga is your family cookbook and shopping list in one app: no ads, no accounts, and your recipes stay on your phone.

RECIPE BOOKS
• Organise your recipes in books: grandma's, a friend's or one per theme, each with its own cover, colour, emoji and a few words about it.
• Complete recipes: ingredients with quantity and unit, formatted steps, photos, times, difficulty, origin, tags, equipment and sub-recipes.
• Adjust the servings and the quantities are recalculated for you.
• Search by name or ingredient and filter by time, difficulty, category, allergens and more. "What can I cook with what I have?" from your pantry.

SAVE RECIPES FROM ANYWHERE
• Paste or share the link to a recipe website, a reel or a video and keep it as just another recipe, with its photo and the author in the source.
• When the recipe is written on the page or in the post's text, Miga reads it without the assistant.
• Take a photo of a recipe in a book or a handwritten one to fill in the editor.
• Ready-to-install recipe packs: world cuisines, rice dishes, baking and more.

FAVOURITES: YOUR COOKS AND WEBSITES
• Follow video channels, profiles and recipe websites, and see what they posted last, with what's new marked.
• On many websites, "More recipes" and a search.
• Save the videos and reels you want to cook and import them with one tap. Posts that aren't about cooking are hidden.

COOKING MODE
• Big steps and the screen always on.
• Several timers that keep going when you leave the app.
• Reading aloud and voice control, hands-free.

SHOPPING LIST
• Add a recipe's ingredients at once, or type, dictate or scan the barcode.
• Sorted by the aisles of your supermarket. Several lists and templates.
• Shop mode: swipe to tick items and the screen stays on. Widget, and the list shared by QR code.
• Open Food Facts product sheet: Nutri-Score, NOVA, allergens and additives.

ALLERGENS AND HEALTH (OPTIONAL)
• The 14 allergens of EU law plus lactose, worked out from the ingredients: show all of them, only yours or none.
• Coeliac mode: what to buy certified and how to avoid cross-contamination.
• Carbohydrate portions per serving and "Fit to my meal" with your plan.
This is a guide only: always check the labels and follow your healthcare team's advice.

COOKING ASSISTANT (OPTIONAL)
With your own key for an AI provider (some offer free models): recipes from photos or from a dish's name, recipes from videos that don't have it written down (it listens to or watches the video), polishing the wording, ideas and menus, and ingredient substitutes. It shows the steps it is really taking. It only works if you add your key, which is stored encrypted on your phone.

SHARING, BACKUPS AND FAMILY
• Share a recipe or a book as an encrypted link, a PDF with a cover, an image or a Miga file.
• Full backups, with photos and optional encryption.
• Sync books and the shopping list with your family through a server you host yourself (miga-server), inviting with a QR code. Each phone chooses which books it wants.

PRIVACY
No ads, no analytics and no accounts. Lock with fingerprint, face or PIN. The internet is only used for what you choose: importing recipes, Favourites, the assistant with your key, Open Food Facts, the pack catalogue and your server.

Light or dark theme, eight accent colours, in English and Spanish. Product data from Open Food Facts (ODbL licence).
